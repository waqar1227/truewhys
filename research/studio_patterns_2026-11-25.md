# YouTube Studio research for our niche (owner pasted 25 Nov 2026)
Sample: 99 videos relevant to our channel, 100M views total. Use these PATTERNS with our OWN topics (never copy these videos' topics, titles or visuals).

1. "How [X] Happened": origin/process framing, often an unlikely or accidental path.
   e.g. How Donuts Were Invented (11.4M) · How a Hole 2.5 Miles Deep Becomes a Gold Mine (7.9M) · How Napoleon Faced an Army Alone (1.8M).
   Why it works: a clear, satisfying explanation of a fascinating process or milestone, with no friction.
2. Counter-intuitive "Why": a peculiar rule, design or historical decision.
   e.g. Why Britain's power grid fears half-time (7.7M) · Why Did Soviet Army Uniforms Remain So Old Fashioned? (5.1M) · Why Landfills Keep Garbage From Breaking Down (1.5M).
   Why it works: a surprising contradiction sparks the need to know the hidden reason.
3. Singular person/object/test spotlight with one vivid defining detail.
   e.g. The Actor Who Invented the Backpack Parachute (4.0M) · The Man Who Created the Original Smiley Face for $45 (4.3M) · This Chinese Exam Took 9 Days in a Tiny Box (6.9M).
   Why it works: an unusual human story or striking relic promises a quick, compelling story.

Takeaways for us: titles in these 3 shapes; one concrete number or detail in the title (9 days, $45, 2.5 miles); stories of real (historical, non-living, non-religious) people and processes;
1 relevant emoji in the title is common. Owner: try our style, Zack-D-like realistic 3D (Style R), and new relevant ideas.
