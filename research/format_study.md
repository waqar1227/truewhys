# Format study: the three model channels (8 Nov 2026)

We copy FORMAT only (length, structure, pacing, topic shapes). Never their titles, scripts, characters, art or exact topics.

## Zack D. Films (Shorts, realistic 3D)
- ~30–60 s "fact" Shorts, nearly 100 % 3D since late 2023; ~29.5 M subscribers; top two "how it works" Shorts ~105–113 M views.
- The first 1–2 s decide it: open on a **shocking visual premise** that stops the scroll before any words land.
- Beat recipe (6–7 beats): hook visual → setup → zoom-in (inside the body/machine) → twist → payoff → closing question.
- Strongest topic shapes: "How X works (inside)", "What happens if…", "Why you should never…".
Sources: [ivideonow](https://ivideonow.com/blog/why-zack-d-films-dominates-youtube-shorts-with-unsettling-3d-animation-content-en),
[fandom](https://youtube.fandom.com/wiki/Zack_D._Films), [taletok](https://taletok.io/zack-d-films-ai-generator/) (vendor pages: treat numbers as approximate).

## Ink Explainer (long videos)
- ~100 % long-form, average ~9:40, about 1 upload per week; topics: human history, behaviour, science of everyday things; sources listed in the description.
- Breakout = one **repeatable series formula** ("What Did Ancient Humans…", top ~14 M views). Evergreen curiosity questions.
- We never do Stone Age / ancient humans (their series). Our equivalent series: "What Did ___ Actually Do All Day?" with a new era/group each time.
Sources: [outlierkit](https://outlierkit.com/channel/inkexplainer96), [YT Wealth Media case study](https://ytwealthmedia.com/case-studies/ink-explainer/), [Social Blade](https://socialblade.com/youtube/handle/inkexplainer96).

## Rick's Clips (Shorts look)
- Started Sep 2025; ~100 K subs, ~264 M views: flat **2D-looking characters on 3D backgrounds**, vertical explainer Shorts.
- This is exactly our Style T look (2D puppets composited on Kaggle-rendered 3D sets).
Source: [NamuWiki](https://en.namu.wiki/w/Ricks%20Clips).

## What we change (applies from now on)
1. **Every Short opens on a shocking visual in the first 1–2 s** (no logo, no "did you know"), with the hook line on screen by 0.5 s.
2. **Shorts length 30–45 s** (cut Shorts over 45 s get tightened); 6–7 beats ending on a question that sends viewers to the long video.
3. **Standalone Shorts batch = Zack-D topic shapes in our toon style**: "How X works inside", "What happens if…", "Why you should never…"
   (body, everyday objects, history mysteries). Style R test Short uses the same shapes in realistic 3D.
4. **Long videos 8–10 min as a repeatable series**: "What Did ___ Actually Do All Day?" (medieval knights, Victorian chimney sweeps,
   pirates, Roman soldiers… never Stone Age), plus Ranked Lists ("8 Worst Jobs…") that cut into Shorts.
5. When one breaks out: next episode in the same series with a new era/group (CLAUDE.md topic-testing rule).
6. Sources always in the description; original cast, music and art only (no copyright risk).

## TikTok location
- TikTok's posting API (and Metricool) has **no location / country targeting**. Location tags exist only in the app and don't choose the audience.
- What does help English-speaking reach: English captions + on-screen text, English hashtags, and **posting at US/UK evening times**
  (US 6–9 pm ET = 4–7 am PKT; UK 6–9 pm GMT = 11 pm–2 am PKT). Don't fake location or use a VPN (against TikTok rules, risks the account).
