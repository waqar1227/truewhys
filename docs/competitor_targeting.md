# Reaching our competitors' audience safely (decided 22 Nov 2026)

## Never (policy / legal risk, little gain)
- No competitor names (Zack D. Films, Rick's Clips, Ink Explainer or any other creator) in titles, tags, descriptions, hashtags, thumbnails or
  pinned comments. YouTube's spam policy bans metadata that tricks viewers about what a video is
  (https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2801973); names are also trademarks/brands, and the impersonation policy covers look-alikes
  (https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2801947). It could cost reach, a warning or a strike, and tags barely affect discovery anyway.
- No hashtags that don't match the video; max 3–5 (more than 60 hashtags → YouTube ignores them all; misleading ones can get the video removed:
  https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6390658).
- No self-promo comments on their videos (spam), no copied scripts, titles, characters, art or edits, no re-uploads, no "reaction" clips.

## Do (how their viewers find us legitimately)
1. **Same viewers, same interests:** YouTube recommends by what viewers watch and finish. Making videos those viewers also love (curiosity
   "why/how" topics, body/science, everyday objects, weird history) puts us next to them in Suggested and the Shorts feed.
2. **Topic radar:** `lib/competitors.py` (in daily.sh) lists their breakouts and fastest growers. Use the TOPIC AREA as a signal and make our own
   researched video with a different angle/object (topics and facts are not owned; their scripts and visuals are).
   Example 22 Nov: Rick's "This Shape Saved Roller Coasters" +3.0 M in 8 days → confirms our L4 shapes angle.
3. **Search words viewers really type:** plain topic keywords in title/description/tags ("why coins have ridges", "stop sign yellow"),
   the question form people search, 10–15 relevant tags, 3 relevant hashtags (#history #science + topic).
4. **Packaging at their level:** the hook in the first 3 s, one clear object, curiosity-gap title patterns (Why…/How…/The … That…), strong
   thumbnails; test their emoji-at-the-end Shorts title style on a same-topic pair before adopting it.
5. **Timing:** post when the shared US/UK audience is active (docs/posting_times.md).
6. **Community:** pinned question comment, reply to comments, teaser posts before long videos.
