# Posting days and times (researched 22 Nov 2026)

## Our audience (YouTube Analytics, 28 days to 22 Nov)
Views by country: US 2724 · GB 1328 · PH 1124 · CA 486 · AU 457 · PL 341 · NL 311 · MY 303. 91 % of views come from the Shorts feed.
The API gives no hour-of-day data; check Studio → Audience → "When your viewers are on YouTube" once the channel has enough views, then update this file.

## Outside studies
- Buffer, 1.8 M videos (2026): Shorts do best on **Fri**, then Sat and Thu (best slot Fri ~4 pm local); long videos do best on **Sun** (~10 am local), then Tue and Mon.
  https://buffer.com/resources/best-time-to-post-on-youtube/
- vidIQ, ~40 M videos: Sun best (+3.5 % 30-day views), Sat next, Wed weakest; the exact hour matters little for 30-day views.
  https://vidiq.com/blog/post/best-time-publish-video-youtube/
- SocialPilot, 301 k videos: Fri 4–7 pm strongest for Shorts; Sunday evening and 3–7 am worst. https://www.socialpilot.co/insights/best-time-to-post-on-youtube
- Several guides: publish 1–4 h before the audience's peak so the video is processed and tested first.

## Decision
- Long: **Sunday 17:00 UTC** (noon US East, 9 am US West, 5 pm UK; weekend daytime, Sun = best long day in both big studies).
- Shorts: **Tue, Thu, Fri, Sat 18:00 UTC** (1 pm US East, 6 pm UK evening; ahead of the US afternoon/evening peak; Fri/Sat/Thu = best Shorts days).
- Free: **Mon, Wed** (Wed weakest day in vidIQ). One post a day at most, ≥ 23 h apart (Sat 18:00 → Sun 17:00 = 23 h).
- Re-check after 4 weeks with our own numbers (views at 48 h by weekday/slot) before changing anything; already-scheduled videos never move.
