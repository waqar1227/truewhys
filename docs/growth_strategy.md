# Growth strategy (22 Nov 2026; owner: "researcher, strategist and performance improver"; nothing risky or against YouTube policy)

## What our own numbers say (research/stats/2026-11-22.md, 38 videos)
1. **Shorts are the engine:** 91 % of views come from the Shorts feed. Long videos get 33–56 views, 11–19 % average viewed, and lose most viewers
   in the first 10 % (retention 0.19–0.44 at 10 %). → Long videos need a stronger first 30 s AND traffic sent from our Shorts.
2. **Winners (1 000–1 700 views):** one everyday object or body mystery + a clear claim (plane windows, quicksand, brain freeze, vending machine,
   hangman gloves), and trending (GTA 6: 1 707 in a day). **Losers (5–200):** old jobs/medieval and the pirate run (7 pirate Shorts in a row).
3. **The first second decides:** winners keep 50–60 % of viewers instead of swiping (stayed %), losers 25–30 %. Relative retention at 10 % is
   mostly under 0.5, so our openings were weaker than similar videos → first-3-seconds rule (owner 22 Nov).
4. **Length:** best average viewed: Brain freeze 34 s (93 %), Hangman 24 s (83 %), Pirate vote 22 s (97 %). → Shorts 22–35 s.
5. **Subscribers:** 19 total; best converters: Quicksand (6), Plane windows (4), Pirate parrots (3) = relatable "you" topics.

## The plan (all policy-safe)
1. **Topics (biggest lever):** each week 2 Shorts = everyday object / body / "would you" topics; 1–2 Shorts = trending now (game launches,
   space, sport, viral news facts; check Google Trends + competitors.py radar), never more than 2 Shorts of one theme in a row. Long videos:
   everyday-object / science angles with search demand (L4 shapes; Rick's "This Shape Saved Roller Coasters" +3 M in 8 days).
2. **Hook system:** frame 1 = the object + a face with emotion + a question in ≤ 4 words on screen; first spoken line states the strange claim
   in ≤ 2 s. No intros, no greetings.
3. **Shorts → long video:** every cut Short gets the long video as "Related video" (owner sets in Studio; API can't); pinned comment on the long
   video asks a question; long video end screen points to the next long video.
4. **Long videos:** cold open from the most dramatic moment, a new mini-mystery every 30–45 s, chapters with search words, 3 thumbnails in
   Test & Compare, specific searchable titles.
5. **Packaging tests, one at a time, same-topic pairs:** emoji at the end of Shorts titles (competitor style) — A-019 vs a later Short;
   thumbnail Test & Compare on every long video; log in topic_results.md.
6. **Accessibility for 21 dubbed languages:** no burned-in captions, SRT on every video, minimal English text in the picture.
7. **Timing:** docs/posting_times.md (long Sun 17:00, Shorts Tue/Thu/Fri/Sat 18:00 UTC).
8. **Measure → decide:** 48 h after each video: views, stayed %, avg % viewed, subs gained; diagnose discovery vs hook vs retention before
   changing anything (growth review session, research/daily_report.md). After 4 weeks: move 80 % of output to the top 3 topic types.

## Never (see docs/competitor_targeting.md)
Other creators' names in metadata, unrelated or excessive hashtags/tags, misleading titles/thumbnails, copied scripts/art/edits, re-uploads,
spam comments, buying views/subs, sub-for-sub, engagement bait ("like if…" lies), fake urgency.
