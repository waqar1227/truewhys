# B-005 C2 (cut Short, rewritten for vertical). Frame 1: hands bending a spaghetti stick that bursts into 3 pieces, "NEVER IN TWO?". Loops to the bend.
H1|0|Try snapping spaghetti into two pieces. You can't.
L01|0|It breaks into three or more, almost every time. Even Richard Feynman couldn't explain why.
L02|0|When the stick snaps in the middle, each half springs back.
L03|0|That sends a bending wave down the pieces... and they snap again.
L04|0|In 2018, a team at MIT found the trick: twist it almost a full turn first, then bend.
L05|0|The twist soaks up the wave, and it breaks cleanly in two. Now try it.
