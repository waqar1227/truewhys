# Sources — A-019 Why Is AMBULANCE Written Backwards? (checked 22 Nov 2026)

1. Mirror lettering on ambulance fronts reads correctly in a driver's rear-view mirror:
   - Wikipedia, "Mirror writing" (use on emergency vehicles) — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mirror_writing
   - Vaia / Hewitt, Conceptual Physics 12e ch. 28 problem 54 (why lettering on the front of some vehicles is "backward") — https://www.vaia.com/en-us/textbooks/physics/conceptual-physics-12-edition/chapter-28/problem-54-why-is-the-lettering-on-the-front-of-some-vehicle
2. A plane mirror swaps left and right as we see it (front-back inversion read as left-right):
   - ScienceABC, "Why do mirrors reverse left-right but not top-bottom" — https://www.scienceabc.com/eyeopeners/mirror-reverse-left-right-not-top-bottom.html
   - Hewitt (above).
3. The US federal ambulance purchase spec requires the front word mirror imaged:
   - GSA KKK-A-1822E §3.16.4 front markings: the word "AMBULANCE", mirror imaged, block, blue, die cut — https://passengermotorvehicles.emilspec.com/KKK-A-1822/page72.html
   - KKK-A-1822A (1980) via Minnesota EMS Regulatory Board — https://mn.gov/oems/assets/KKK-A-1822A_tcm1221-740512.pdf
Not claimed: who invented it or the first year (sources disagree).
