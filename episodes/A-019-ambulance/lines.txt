H1|0|Why is ambulance written backwards?
L01|0|It's not a mistake. It's written for one person: the driver stuck right in front of it.
L02|0|When a siren screams behind you, you don't turn around. You glance up... at your mirror.
L03|0|And a mirror flips everything left to right. A normal word would look backwards.
L04|0|So the word is printed flipped. The official US ambulance spec even requires it.
L05|0|Backwards on the van... but in your mirror, it reads AMBULANCE. Did you ever notice?
