# Sources — A-022 padlock drain hole (checked 22 Nov 2026)
1. The small hole at the bottom of many padlocks is a drain: water/moisture that gets in (around the shackle) can escape instead of causing rust/corrosion,
   and it prevents water freezing inside in cold weather:
   - House Digest, "What Is The Tiny Hole On The Bottom Of A Padlock For?" — https://www.housedigest.com/1809428/padlock-tiny-hole-bottom-use-explained-what-for
   - Monroe Engineering, "Why Padlocks Have a Small Hole on the Bottom" — https://monroeengineering.com/blog/?p=19420
   - Kenyans.co.ke, "3 Functions of Tiny Hole at the Bottom of Padlocks" — https://www.kenyans.co.ke/news/88743-3-functions-tiny-hole-bottom-padlocks
2. Secondary use (not in the script): lubricant can be applied through it (House Digest, Monroe). Not every padlock has the hole on the bottom; some on the side (forum note) → no "always".
