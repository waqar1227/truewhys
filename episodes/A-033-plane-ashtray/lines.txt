H1|0|Why does a no-smoking plane toilet have an ashtray?
L01|0|Smoking on every US flight has been banned since 2000... yet the rules still demand an ashtray on every toilet door.
L02|0|Because the real danger isn't the smoker. It's where the cigarette ends up.
L03|0|Someone who breaks the rule panics and hides it, and the nearest hiding spot is a bin full of paper towels.
L04|0|So the rule gives them one obvious, safe place to put it out first.
L05|0|That bin even has its own tiny fire extinguisher, and a smoke detector watches the room.
L06|0|So this ashtray isn't for smokers... it's for the fire nobody wants.
