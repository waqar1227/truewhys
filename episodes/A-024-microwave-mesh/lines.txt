H1|0|Why is there a metal net in your microwave door?
L01|0|It's not decoration. Without it, the microwaves would pour straight out through the glass.
L02|0|Microwaves are waves, like light, but much longer: about twelve centimetres from crest to crest.
L03|0|The holes in that metal mesh are just a few millimetres wide. Far too small for a wave that big. So the waves bounce back inside.
L04|0|But light waves are hundreds of thousands of times smaller. They slip through easily.
L05|0|That's why you can watch your food... while the microwaves stay locked in.
