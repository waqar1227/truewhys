H1|0|Why do reindeer eyes turn blue in winter?
L01|0|In summer they shine gold. In winter... deep blue.
L02|0|Behind each eye is a mirror that bounces light back, for a second chance to see.
L03|0|But in the Arctic winter the sun hides for weeks, so their pupils stay wide open.
L04|0|Fluid can't drain properly. Pressure builds, and squeezes the mirror's tiny fibres together.
L05|0|Packed tighter, it reflects blue, the colour of twilight. Less light escapes, so they see more in the dark.
