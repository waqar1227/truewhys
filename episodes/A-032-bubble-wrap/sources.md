# A-032 sources (checked 25 Nov 2026)
- 1957: engineers Alfred Fielding and Marc Chavannes (Hawthorne, New Jersey) sealed two shower curtains together, trapping air bubbles, and tried
  to sell the result as textured wallpaper; it failed:
  - Wikipedia, "Bubble wrap" — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bubble_wrap
  - Mental Floss, "The original purpose of bubble wrap" — https://www.mentalfloss.com/history/original-purpose-bubble-wrap
  - Swiss Federal Institute of Intellectual Property (IGE), "From wallpaper to packaging: bubble wrap turns 70" — https://www.ige.ch/en/blog/blog-article/from-wallpaper-to-packaging-bubble-wrap-turns-70
  - The Star (Malaysia) fact check, "Was bubble wrap originally marketed as wallpaper?" (true) — https://www.thestar.com.my/news/true-or-not/2022/07/19/quickcheck-was-bubble-wrap-originally-marketed-as-wallpaper
- They next tried it as greenhouse insulation; that did not succeed either: Wikipedia "Bubble wrap"; Mental Floss (above); IGE (above).
- A computer maker (IBM, for its new 1401 computer, c. 1959–61) used it to protect the machine in shipping; packaging became the use that took off:
  Wikipedia "Bubble wrap" / "Bubble Wrap (brand)" — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bubble_Wrap_(brand) ; Mental Floss (above); IGE (above).
  The exact year differs between sources (1959/1960/1961), so the video says only "then".
- Not claimed: that people "hated" the wallpaper (cut from the narration); the company and computer names are left out of the picture.
