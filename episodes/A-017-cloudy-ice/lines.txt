H1|0|She filled the tray with clear water. So why is the ice cloudy?
L01|0|Water carries air you can't see, dissolved inside it.
L02|0|But ice has almost no room for air.
L03|0|In a freezer, the cube freezes from the outside in.
L04|0|Each new layer of ice pushes the air towards the middle.
L05|0|The middle freezes last, so the air has nowhere to go.
L06|0|It's trapped as tiny bubbles, and they scatter the light. That's the cloud.
L07|0|A still lake freezes from the top down. The air escapes, so lake ice can be clear.
L08|0|That's the trick behind perfectly clear ice: freeze it from one side only.
L09|0|So that cloud in your drink is just air that couldn't get out. Did you know?
