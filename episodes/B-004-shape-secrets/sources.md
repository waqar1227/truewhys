## Stop signs (ch 3)
- 1923 Mississippi Valley Association of State Highway Departments: more sides = more danger; circle = railroad crossing, octagon = stop, diamond = caution,
  square = care, rectangle = information; stop signs yellow (red/black legend) 1924–1954 because no fade-resistant red; Sept 1954 MUTCD → white on red:
  - FHWA, "MUTCD history" — https://mutcd.fhwa.dot.gov/kno-history.htm
  - Wikipedia, "Stop sign" — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stop_sign
  - 99% Invisible, "How safety shaped the octagonal stop sign" — https://99percentinvisible.org/article/red-white-sometimes-blue-how-safety-shaped-the-octagonal-stop-sign/
  - Kittelson MUTCD history (1954 revision, durable red finishes) — https://mutcd.kittelson.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/04/MUTCDhistory3.pdf
