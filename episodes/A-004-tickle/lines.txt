# A-004 v1 (10 Nov 2026), standalone Short, Style T.
H1|0|Try to tickle yourself. Nothing happens. Why?
L01|0|Tickling needs one thing: surprise.
L02|0|Every time you move, a part of your brain called the cerebellum predicts exactly what you'll feel...
L03|0|and turns that feeling down. So your own fingers barely register.
L04|0|Scientists tested this with a robot. People moved one arm, and the robot copied the move onto their other hand.
L05|0|With no delay, it barely tickled. But add a delay of just a fifth of a second...
L06|0|and it started to tickle again. The brain's prediction was off.
L07|0|Your brain ignores what it expects, so it can notice everything else. So, who's ticklish now?
