H1|0|Why does your tongue stick to a frozen pole?
L01|0|Because your own spit just turned into glue.
L02|0|Metal steals heat far faster than wood or plastic. Touch it, and the warmth drains out of your wet tongue in a flash.
L03|0|The spit freezes solid, into the tiny bumps of your tongue and the tiny pits in the metal. Now ice locks them together.
L04|0|Pull hard, and that ice can tear skin right off.
L05|0|So never pull. Pour on warm water, the ice glue melts... and you're free.
